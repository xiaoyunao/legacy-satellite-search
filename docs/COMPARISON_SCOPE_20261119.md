# Comparison scope and remaining physical checks

This is a scientific acceptance note, not manuscript text. The frozen detector, original 18 recovery truths and registered 144 physical trials are unchanged.

## Published census versus compact systems

Tan et al.'s main population excludes circularized radii below 15 pc, but Appendix A explicitly examines compact systems. Thus a compact search is not automatically a novel parameter-space discovery. Appendix B also includes image simulations with resolved and diffuse light; catalogue-only completeness cannot simply be transferred to the most compact regime. Our measured missing faint-light fraction makes that comparison operational: assess its effect in a small paired image test, separately from the 30 pc spatial truncation. Preserve the 144 original trials and label any additional guard experiment separately. [Tan et al., Appendix A–B](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12313v2)

The available published PS1 DR1 classifier remains a separate benchmark. Its thresholds, sample and photometry differ from our internally cost-calibrated PS1 DR2 comparison. Neither its prediction nor a response conditional on potentially contaminated LS proxies is an empirically validated three-arm physical experiment. [Drlica-Wagner et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1912.03302)

## Euclid context update

The November Euclid DR1 study reports three halo systems: Ursa Minor II/Euclid UFSS DR1 1, Fornax 7 and Eridanus V, at about 20, 145 and 166 kpc. Add their Table 1–2 positions to known-context matching before calling any overlapping peak new. Table 2 radii are semi-major; our additional CSV preserves them and also computes circularized values using the reported ellipticity. Ursa Minor II must not be confused with Ursa Major II/III. [Euclid DR1, Tables 1–2 and Figure 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.16025v1)

Fornax 7 also has a dedicated study with different structural/stellar-population estimates. Keep each study's parameter set intact; the new context CSV adopts the single November table consistently, rather than mixing its distance and ellipticity with September values. The available photometry does not establish whether the system is a star cluster or dwarf galaxy. [Fornax-7 study](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14535v1)

## What is still needed

- Exact production WCS-area union and census overlap, distinct from the interim incremental area clock; measured physical-gain area cannot be equated with all searched sky.
- All 144 native trials and the fixed two-arm blind/C measurements, with actual luminosity fluctuations and full source-stage denominators.
- Faint blue-star PS1 response validation or quantitative support/contamination bounds; the current faint Gaia-labelled anchors are red, and shape-cut sensitivity is not an identified contamination fraction.
- A bounded image check of the 30 pc finite window and the unresolved faint light. Do not relabel 68–94% represented V-proxy light as a full-IMF pixel simulation, or infer the direction of the recovery bias without testing it.
- Final same-record C, original-75 crosswalk, probability sampling and anonymous material QA before the user's one formal grading session.
